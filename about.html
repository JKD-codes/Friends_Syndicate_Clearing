About Friends Syndicate Clearing Pvt. Ltd.

An Indian customs brokerage and multimodal supply chain logistics enterprise founded on trust, compliance, and dedicated client service.

Our Inception & Growth

20+ Years of Proven Operational Track Record

Established in 2006 under the visionary stewardship of Managing Director Mr. V. R. Divakaran, Friends Syndicate Clearing Private Limited (FSCPL) has grown into a premier tier-one Customs House Agent and total multimodal freight forwarding enterprise.

Holding official Customs Broker License C. H. A. 11/435 and permanent company tax registration PAN: AABCF0413G, our operations are fortified by AEO certification, granting green-channel priority clearance and fast-track processing across Indian ports.

With our corporate headquarters located at 208, Sahar Cargo Estate, Andheri (E), Mumbai right adjacent to Mumbai International Airport's air cargo complex, and branch offices in Seawoods (Navi Mumbai), Kandla, Mundra, and Chennai, we provide comprehensive nationwide coverage with over 140 logistics specialists across our group.

Corporate Philosophy

Our Vision, Mission & Core Values

Built upon an uncompromising standard of compliance, integrity, and proactive customer updates.

Our Vision

To provide personalized, one-stop logistics services to our customers to their utmost satisfaction. To be recognized as India's most dependable and compliant customs brokerage and multimodal transport partner.

Our Mission

To bridge global manufacturers and Indian importers with zero border friction, providing rapid customs clearance, guaranteed daily status updates, and safe inland haulage via our self-owned 53-truck fleet.
